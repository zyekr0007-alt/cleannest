Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our cleaning approach, booking and guarantee.

Are your cleaning products safe for my family and pets?
Yes. At CleanNest we use eco-friendly products that are safe for your home and the environment. This ensures a thorough clean without compromising on health — ideal for families and pet owners.
What if I'm not happy with the cleaning?
We pride ourselves on our commitment to customer satisfaction. If for any reason you’re not completely satisfied with our service, we will return to resolve the issue at no extra charge.
Which cities do you serve?
We serve Jalandhar, Phagwara, Kapurthala, Hoshiarpur, Nakodar, Goraya, Adampur, Kartarpur, Sultanpur Lodhi, Nawanshahr, Phillaur and Ludhiana, plus nearby cities across Punjab. Message us on WhatsApp with your location and we’ll confirm availability right away.
How do I book and pay?
Message us on WhatsApp, call us, or use the booking form. We’ll send an exact quote, agree on a time, and you approve before we start. No booking fee and no advance payment required.
What's included in a deep clean?
A deep clean goes top to bottom: walls, fans, windows, furniture and floors, plus kitchen degreasing and bathroom sanitisation. Tell us your space on WhatsApp and we’ll confirm exactly what’s covered for your home.
Do I need to provide products or equipment?
No. We bring the professional equipment and eco-friendly cleaning products needed for the job. You don’t need to arrange anything.
Is carpet & steam cleaning safe for kids and pets?
Yes. Our advanced hot-water extraction steam cleaning removes 99% of bacteria, dust mites and deep-set dirt, and is safe for children, pets and sensitive skin. Carpets dry quickly too.
When can you come?
We offer flexible scheduling to accommodate your busy lifestyle — one-time deep clean or regular maintenance, available 7 days a week, including before or after office hours for commercial spaces.
How is the price decided?
Every home is different, so every job is quoted individually — based on the size of the space, its condition and the services you need. You get an exact price on WhatsApp and approve it before we start. No hidden charges, ever.
