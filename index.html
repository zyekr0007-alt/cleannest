What’s included in a full-house deep clean?

Every room, top to bottom: dusting ceilings, fans, walls and fixtures, kitchen degreasing, bathroom descaling, floor scrubbing and mopping, plus window sills and switchboards. Most 1–4 BHK homes take 3–6 hours.

Are your products safe for kids and pets?

Yes — we use professional, eco-friendly products that leave no harsh smells. Every cleaner is verified, trained and uniformed.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

Not necessarily — many customers share keys or schedule a time when someone is home. We’ll work out whatever is most convenient for you.
