Why Your AC Needs Professional Cleaning Every Season

Blog

Your air conditioner works hard 8 months of the year in Jalandhar's heat. But when was the last time it was actually cleaned — not just the filter, but the coils, the fan, the drain line?

Dirty AC = Higher Electricity Bills

When dust and grime coat your AC coils, the compressor works harder to cool your room. A dirty AC can consume 15–20% more electricity. Professional cleaning restores efficiency and saves you money every month.

Dirty AC = Bad Air Quality

Your AC circulates air through your entire home. If the coils, fan, and drain pan are covered in mold and bacteria, you are breathing that in. Professional AC cleaning removes allergens, mold spores, and dust mites from the system.

How Often Should You Clean Your AC?

We recommend professional AC cleaning at least once per season — before summer and before winter. If you use your AC year-round, every 3–4 months is ideal. CleanNest offers both standard cleaning (₹490) and snow foam deep jet cleaning (₹690) per unit.

What Professional AC Cleaning Includes

A professional AC clean includes: coil cleaning with chemical spray, fan blade degreasing, filter wash or replacement, drain line flush, condenser unit exterior wipe, and a performance check. It takes 45–60 minutes per unit.
