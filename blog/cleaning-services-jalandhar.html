What cleaning costs in Jalandhar, how to spot the best and cheapest quotes without getting burned, and the questions to ask before you book a uniformed team.

What people mean by “cleaning services” in Jalandhar

There is no single job called cleaning. In Jalandhar the phrase usually means one of a handful of different services, and each is priced on a different unit. A quote only means something once you know which one you asked for.

  • A full-home deep clean — every room, priced per home by size.
  • A single-room deep clean — kitchen, bathroom or balcony only, priced per room.
  • Upholstery and soft furnishings — sofa priced per seat, mattress per mattress, carpet and blinds per square foot, curtains per panel.
  • Appliance cleaning — air conditioner, chimney, refrigerator, gas stove and exhaust fan, priced per unit.
  • Floor renewal — scrubbing, polishing and marble or granite care, priced per square foot.
  • Post-construction and post-renovation — priced per square foot, because the volume of debris sets the work.
  • Commercial and recurring cleaning — offices, shops and regular visits, priced per square foot or per visit.

Combining two is normal. A full-home deep clean with sofa and mattress cleaning is a common booking; a kitchen deep clean on its own is just as common. The service directory lists every category with its scope.

Why two quotes for the same flat differ by thousands

When quotes for the same 3 BHK come back thousands apart, it is almost never because one company is dishonest. It is usually one of these seven things, and every one of them is worth asking about directly.

  1. Size is counted differently. A “3 BHK” can mean 1,100 sq ft or 1,600 sq ft. Ask whether the quote is based on your carpet area or on a generic per-BHK average.
  2. Condition drives labour more than size does. A flat deep cleaned three months ago and a flat handed over after renovation are the same size and completely different jobs. Grease, cement residue and years of deposit cost time, not area.
  3. The scope is different. The expensive differences hide in the details: are cabinet interiors included, or only the fronts? Is the chimney separate? Are window tracks included or only the glass? Is upholstery a separate line?
  4. Access is different. A lift, parking near the door and a working water connection save an hour. Three floors of stairs, no parking and a dry tap add one.
  5. Team size and time are different. Four hours with two people and four hours with five people are not the same amount of work. Ask for the team size alongside the duration.
  6. Consumables are different. Confirm whether products, machines and tools are in the price or added afterwards.
  7. Travel is different. Inside Jalandhar city and a town 30 km out are different trips. Confirm travel before booking, not on the day.

So when two quotes differ, the useful question is not “why are you more expensive”. It is “send me your scope list and I will compare the two line by line”. A company that cannot produce a written scope is the one to worry about, not the one with the higher number.

What a full-home deep clean includes — and what it does not

A full-home deep clean covers the surfaces of every room, worked top to bottom. A standard scope includes:

  • Interior dusting and wipe-down of furniture and accessible surfaces
  • Kitchen deep clean — countertop, stove, degreasing
  • Bathroom descaling, sanitising and fittings polish
  • Floor vacuuming and mopping throughout
  • Windows, mirrors and glass
  • Appliance exteriors
  • Skirting boards, switchboards and door frames

The exclusions matter just as much, because they are where most disagreements start. These are not part of a deep clean by default:

  • Washing or repainting interior walls
  • Any repair — plumbing, electrical or carpentry. A leaking tap, damaged grout or peeling cabinet finish needs a tradesperson, not a cleaner.
  • Pest control
  • Moving heavy furniture, or cleaning behind fixed and built-in units
  • Exterior glass that cannot be reached safely
  • Chimney interiors during a kitchen clean — chimney cleaning is a separate item
  • Mould that has worked into plaster or behind tiles. Surface mould is a cleaning job; mould inside the substrate is a repair.

Ask any company for its exclusion list before you book. A quote with no exclusions written down has not told you anything yet.

What actually decides how long it takes

No honest company can quote a duration before it knows your home, and a company that gives you a confident number over the phone without asking any questions is guessing. What moves the clock:

  • Usable floor area, not the BHK label
  • How long since the last deep clean, and what has built up since
  • Kitchen grease and bathroom hard-water deposit
  • How much has to be emptied or moved to reach a surface
  • Number of bathrooms, balconies and window tracks in scope
  • Whether upholstery, mattresses or carpets are added
  • Water and power availability, and how far the team has to carry equipment

Ask for the time window and the team size together. “Four hours” from a two-person team and “four hours” from a five-person team are different amounts of work, and comparing them without the team size tells you nothing.

Ten questions to ask before you book anyone

This list is the whole point of the guide. It works on any cleaning company in Jalandhar, and it works on CleanNest too.

  1. Can you quote my actual flat, based on my carpet area, rather than an average for its BHK?
  2. Can I have the scope in writing — what is included, and what is excluded — before I pay anything?
  3. Who does the work: your own trained team, or labour arranged on the day?
  4. Which of my surfaces need a test patch first — marble, wooden flooring, fabric, upholstery?
  5. What would it cost to add the items that are excluded?
  6. What happens if something inside the agreed scope is missed?
  7. Is the advance refundable, and until when?
  8. Are water, electricity, parking and travel included in the price?
  9. Which products and machines will be used, and are they safe around children and pets?
  10. Can I see the completion list before I pay the balance?

Answers to these give you a scope, a price, a person responsible and a remedy. That is the entire basis of a fair booking.

Red flags worth walking away from

  • No written scope — only a number in a chat, with nothing itemised.
  • A large cash advance with no refund terms — ask what happens if you cancel, and get the answer before paying, not after.
  • A price far below every other quote. It usually means fewer people, less time, or a scope that quietly leaves out the expensive parts. The gap reappears as an extra on the day.
  • No test patch on fabric, marble or wood flooring before treatment. These surfaces can be permanently marked by the wrong method.
  • A company that cannot tell you its own rates. If the price depends on who answers the phone, it is not a rate card.
  • Reluctance to confirm identity or address. A listed business address and a verifiable phone number are the minimum.

Best, cheapest, easiest to book — what those three words actually mean

Three phrases come up constantly in searches for cleaning in Jalandhar, and each hides a different question. They are worth separating, because the answer you should trust is different for each one.

“Best cleaning service in Jalandhar”

Best at what? Nobody can verify best from a website, including this one. A rating is an average of other people's jobs, in other people's homes, at other budgets. What you can verify in a single message is whether a company will put its scope in writing, say who is doing the work, and tell you what happens if something inside that scope is missed. A company that answers the ten questions above is a better bet than one with a higher rating and no answers.

“Cheapest cleaning service in Jalandhar”

The cheapest published rate rarely produces the cheapest job. Price comes out of four things: how many people come, how long they stay, how many of the awkward parts of the scope are included, and whether the machines and products are in the price. A low quote that leaves out cabinet interiors, window tracks and the chimney is not cheaper — it is a smaller job at a similar rate. Compare scope first, then compare the number. Our own published rates are on the rate card; use them as the baseline you measure other quotes against, including ours.

“Easiest to book”, “quickest to respond”

This one is genuinely testable, and it takes ten minutes. Message a company during working hours and watch two things: how long the reply takes, and whether it arrives with a scope and a price rather than a request to call. Then ask whether the date is actually held, what the advance is, and whether moving the date costs anything. A company that answers all of that in its first reply is easy to book. One that needs three follow-ups before it will name a number is not, however good its work turns out to be.

CleanNest answers enquiries within 1–2 hours during working hours (9 AM–8 PM, seven days a week), sends the scope and price in that reply, takes a free quote with no booking fee, and lets you reschedule at no charge up to 24 hours before the visit.

Using a Reddit thread to shortlist a cleaner

Search for a cleaner in Jalandhar and you will find recommendation threads on Reddit and in local Facebook groups. They are worth reading, and worth reading carefully, because a recommendation is not a quotation.

What threads are good for: names. They tell you which companies exist locally and are still being used by real people, which is genuinely useful when every company's own website says the same things.

What a thread cannot tell you:

  • Someone else's flat is not yours. A recommendation from a 1 BHK with one bathroom says nothing about a 4 BHK with three, or about a home that has not had a deep clean in four years.
  • Threads go stale. A company recommended in 2022 may have changed hands, changed staff, or stopped taking single-room jobs.
  • Price almost never appears. People post “highly recommended”, not “₹11,400 for a 2 BHK including the balcony”. Without the number and the size, the recommendation does not transfer to your job.
  • Some recommendations are the company. Astroturfing is common in local threads and it is not always obvious. One glowing comment from an account with no other history is not evidence.

So use a thread to build a shortlist of two or three names, then put the ten questions to each of them. The thread gives you the names; the questions give you the decision.

And if you are the one posting, ask for two details with every recommendation: the size of the home and the price actually paid. Those are the only parts of the reply that transfer to your job.
