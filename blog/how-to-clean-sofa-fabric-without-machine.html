How to Clean Sofa Fabric Without a Machine

Cleaning tips and guides for Jalandhar homes & offices — by the team rated 4.9☆ on Google.

Updated 15 Aug 2026 Jalandhar, Punjab CleanNest Team

Weekly sofa care

  • Vacuum the whole sofa — cushions, seams and under the cushions — weekly.
  • Use the upholstery attachment to lift dust and crumbs.
  • Flip and rotate cushions regularly for even wear.

Spot cleaning without a machine

  • Blot spills immediately with a clean cloth.
  • For stains: dab with mild soap solution, rinse with a damp cloth, blot dry.
  • For odours: sprinkle baking soda, let it sit 15 minutes, vacuum thoroughly.

The honest truth

  • Home methods maintain a sofa but can't deep-clean the inner fabric.
  • Over months, body oils, dust and odour build up inside.
  • A professional sofa dry clean every 4–6 months keeps it fresh — and usually cheaper than replacing the sofa.
