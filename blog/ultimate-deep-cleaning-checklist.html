CLEANING JOURNAL

The Ultimate Deep Cleaning Checklist for Every Room

A practical room-by-room checklist covering preparation, kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms and living spaces, with material-care limits and booking guidance.

Deep cleaning and routine cleaning

A deep clean focuses on areas that routine wiping and sweeping can miss: edges, buildup, accessible fixtures and the surfaces behind everyday belongings. It does not mean every object needs the strongest chemical or that every appliance should be dismantled. Use this checklist to identify the work your home needs and agree a manageable scope.

Separate cleaning from repairs. A leaking tap, damaged grout or peeling cabinet finish needs attention beyond removing dirt. Mark those issues before you start, and keep manufacturer care instructions available for delicate surfaces.

Prepare before moving room to room

  • Put away documents, valuables and loose objects so surfaces are accessible.
  • Identify fragile finishes and furniture that cannot be moved safely.
  • Choose products suitable for each material and follow their labels. Do not mix cleaning products.
  • Keep separate cloths for kitchen and bathroom tasks, and plan ventilation and drying.
  • Work through one area at a time, with floors after the surfaces above them.

Kitchen Deep Cleaning Checklist

  • Wipe cabinet fronts, handles and accessible tops; empty interiors if you plan to clean them.
  • Clean the hob, backsplash, counter and sink using material-appropriate care.
  • Check chimney filters and book a separate chimney clean if needed.
  • Remove food before cleaning refrigerator shelves and drawers.
  • Wipe appliance exteriors and accessible window tracks.
  • Clean the floor and edges without forcing heavy appliances or disturbing gas connections.

Keep appliance interiors distinct from the general room scope. Our kitchen service page explains the included hob and exhaust work and the optional chimney service.

Bathroom Deep Cleaning Checklist

  • Remove toiletries and loose floor items.
  • Clean tiles, grout, basin and fittings after checking finish compatibility.
  • Wipe mirrors and shower glass; avoid improvised blades or abrasive tools on coatings.
  • Clean the toilet with dedicated tools.
  • Check accessible drain areas and report slow drainage or leaks.
  • Dry wet surfaces and note any recurring damp patches.

White deposits, etching and worn plating may look alike. Do not assume a mark can be scrubbed away. See our bathroom cleaning scope for realistic treatment expectations.

Bedroom Deep Cleaning Checklist

  • Launder bedding and protectors according to their labels.
  • Dust accessible shelves, bedside tables, door frames and skirting.
  • Clean under the bed where access allows, without lifting unsafe loads.
  • Check mattress care instructions before vacuuming, wet treatment or turning it.
  • Wipe wardrobe surfaces after removing belongings.
  • Review curtains, blinds and fabric items for separate cleaning needs.

Leave enough time for any treated fabric to dry before replacing bedding or closing storage spaces.

Living Room Deep Cleaning Checklist

  • Dust accessible shelves, frames and decorative items.
  • Vacuum upholstery and cushion creases if the care instructions permit.
  • Inspect rug and sofa labels before selecting any wet or steam treatment.
  • Clean window sills and tracks and wipe switches carefully.
  • Use device-manufacturer instructions for screens and electronics.
  • Finish with floor edges and accessible space beneath furniture.

Turn the checklist into an agreed job

For a professional visit, send room counts, photos of problem areas and a list of optional specialist work. Ask which inaccessible areas are excluded, how long the agreed scope is expected to take and what you should prepare. Review the completed areas with the team before putting belongings back.
