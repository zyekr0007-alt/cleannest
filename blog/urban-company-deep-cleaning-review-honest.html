Urban Company Deep Cleaning Review: What They Don't Tell You

Blog

Urban Company (UrbanClap) spends heavily on marketing and app store ratings. But what do real customers say after the service? We analyzed hundreds of reviews to bring you the truth.

The Good: Convenience

Urban Company's app is easy to use. Booking takes 2 minutes. Payment is digital. For a one-time booking in a pinch, it works. The platform also offers a damage guarantee of up to ₹10,000, which provides some peace of mind.

The Bad: Reliability

This is the #1 complaint across hundreds of Trustpilot reviews. Cleaners cancel on the day of service. They arrive late. They leave early. The support team offers only rescheduling — no accountability, no compensation for your wasted time.

The Ugly: Hidden Upsells

Many customers report that the quoted price is not the final price. Technicians arrive and immediately upsell — "your AC needs special chemical cleaning" or "your sofa needs an extra treatment." You either pay more or get incomplete work. CleanNest quotes the final price before we arrive. No upsells, no surprises.

The Bottom Line

Urban Company is a decent option for metro cities where nothing else is available. But in Jalandhar, where CleanNest offers better pricing, consistent quality, and direct accountability, there is no reason to gamble on a platform.
