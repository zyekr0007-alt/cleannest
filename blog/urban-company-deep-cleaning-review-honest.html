CleanNest and Urban Company: Comparing Cleaning Quotes

Use a scope-first checklist to compare cleaning quotes, included work, unit pricing and booking conditions without relying on unsupported platform claims.

CleanNest Cleaning JournalUpdated 12 September 2026

Compare the same job scope

A useful comparison starts with the property, room count and work required. Write down kitchen, bathrooms, floors and furnishings separately, then ask each provider to state what is included. Similar service names do not necessarily describe the same job.

This guide does not claim to be a customer review of Urban Company or a current audit of its prices. Request its current quote and terms directly for your location; no competitor price or availability is asserted here.

Starting prices and optional work

Check whether the quote is per home, per seat, per unit or measured area. Ask which conditions change the price and whether specialist work is separate. CleanNest’s published rate card lists ranges and units; the final scope and price are confirmed before booking.

For CleanNest, chimney cleaning is an optional separate selection, even with a kitchen clean. Appliance exteriors differ from internal servicing, and fabric treatments need material confirmation. Apply the same specific questions to any quote you compare.

Booking, payment and support questions

Ask when a date becomes confirmed, how to request changes, what payment is due and how an issue is reported. Read the applicable policies before paying. CleanNest takes 30% to reserve an agreed service date and the remaining 70% after service. The advance is refundable for cancellations at least two days (48 hours) before the confirmed start; with less notice it is non-refundable. Its online estimate alone does not reserve a slot.

Record the agreement before choosing

Keep the itemised scope, exclusions, confirmed price and contact route together. Choose based on the work and terms you can verify, not an unsubstantiated claim that one provider always performs better. Start a CleanNest estimate and ask the team to confirm the details for your space.
