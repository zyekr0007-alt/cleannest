How to Degrease a Kitchen: Step-by-Step

Cleaning tips and guides for Jalandhar homes & offices — by the team rated 4.9☆ on Google.

Updated 15 Aug 2026 Jalandhar, Punjab CleanNest Team

Why grease is stubborn

  • Indian cooking — oil, tadka, frying — deposits a sticky film on every nearby surface.
  • Grease hardens over time and attracts dust, making it worse.
  • Ordinary dish soap won't dissolve cooked-on grease.

Step-by-step degreasing

  • Step 1: Apply a degreaser (alkaline, kitchen-safe) to tiles, stove and chimney filter.
  • Step 2: Let it sit 5–10 minutes to break down the grease.
  • Step 3: Scrub with a non-scratch sponge or brush.
  • Step 4: Rinse with clean water and wipe dry.
