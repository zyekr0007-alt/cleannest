# CleanNest Homepage — Structure Reference

> Step 2 of the homepage redesign project.
> This document locks the homepage section order and basic structure ONLY.
> Detailed section design happens in later steps, one prompt at a time.

## Flow

INTRODUCE → BUILD TRUST → SHOW SERVICES → SHOW RESULTS → EXPLAIN VALUE →
EXPLAIN PROCESS → PROVIDE SOCIAL PROOF → SHOW LOCATION → CONVERT → FOOTER

## Section Order (10 sections, verified in index.html)

| # | Section | Anchor/ID | Purpose | Contents (basic) |
|---|---------|-----------|---------|------------------|
| 01 | HERO | — | Immediately explain what CleanNest does; encourage a quote | Main headline · short supporting text · primary CTA (quote form → WhatsApp) · secondary CTA (WhatsApp Us) · main cleaning visual (real work photo + rating/location badges) |
| 02 | TRUST MARQUEE | — | Build immediate trust | Scrolling strip: ★ 4.9 Google rating · professional team · professional equipment · transparent pricing · residential & commercial · Jalandhar & nearby |
| 03 | SERVICES | `#services` | Quickly show main services | Card/grid of 9 services · image · short description · "Get a price" action · category filter chips |
| 04 | BEFORE & AFTER | `#results` | Visually demonstrate work quality | Real CleanNest job photos · grayscale→colour reveal (hover/tap) · short heading |
| 05 | WHY CLEANNEST | `#why` | Why choose CleanNest over competitors | 3 short advantage cards with icons: transparent pricing · experts only · 100% quality assured |
| 06 | HOW IT WORKS | `#how` | Make booking easy to understand | 3 steps: 01 Choose your service → 02 Get your quote → 03 We clean & you relax |
| 07 | CUSTOMER REVIEWS | `#reviews` | Build trust with real feedback | Real Google reviews only · star ratings · customer names · Google indication · horizontal marquee |
| 08 | SERVICE AREAS | `#areas` | Show where CleanNest operates | Compact chips: Jalandhar + nearby cities currently served |
| 09 | FINAL CTA | — | Convert bottom-of-page visitors | Strong closing headline · short supporting text · Get Free Quote · WhatsApp Us |
| 10 | FOOTER | — | Essential business info + navigation | Logo · short description · contact · WhatsApp · important links · services · service areas · copyright |

## Supporting (non-section) structure

- Sticky header: logo, nav (Home/Services/About/Blog/FAQ/Contact), "Get Free Quote" button
- Mobile sticky action bar: Call + WhatsApp
- Skip-link (accessibility), reveal animations, smooth scroll

## Constraints

- Keep homepage short and one-page.
- No additional homepage sections without approval.
- No repeated information between sections.
- Real reviews only — never invent customer feedback.
- Reuse existing CleanNest branding, assets, and functionality.
- Check desktop + mobile responsiveness after every step.
