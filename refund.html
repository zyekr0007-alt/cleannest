Refund & Satisfaction Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Our satisfaction guarantee

We stand behind every clean. If you are not completely satisfied with our work, tell us within 48 hours of the service. We will return and re-clean the affected areas at no extra charge — this is our promise to you.

How to request a re-clean

  • Message us on WhatsApp at +91 76100 00654 within 48 hours of the service.
  • Tell us which areas did not meet your expectation.
  • We will schedule a return visit at the earliest available slot.

Refunds

  • No advance payment is taken at the time of booking — payment is agreed and settled around the time of service.
  • If a booked service is cancelled by us (for example, we cannot attend on the agreed date), we will reschedule or refund any amount already paid, in full, within 7 working days.
  • If a service could not be delivered because access was not provided or the booking was cancelled by the customer, any amount already paid may be credited toward a future booking or refunded at our discretion.
  • Refunds are processed via the same method used for payment where possible.

Damages

Our staff take great care with your property. In the unlikely event that an item is damaged during cleaning, report it within 48 hours with photos and we will work with you to resolve it fairly — repair or appropriate compensation.

Contact

Any concerns: WhatsApp +91 76100 00654 or email cleannestclub@gmail.com. We respond quickly and treat every issue seriously.
