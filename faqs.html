What services do you offer?

Full house deep cleaning, kitchen deep cleaning, bathroom deep cleaning, sofa dry cleaning, carpet & steam cleaning, AC services, chimney cleaning and commercial cleaning.

Are your cleaning products safe for kids and pets?

Yes — we use professional, eco-friendly products that leave no harsh smells and are safe for your family and pets.

Not necessarily. Many customers share keys or schedule when someone is home. We'll work out what's most convenient for you.

Nothing much — we bring all equipment and supplies. Just clear valuables and fragile items, and we'll handle the rest.

How long does a deep cleaning take?

A typical home deep cleaning takes 3–6 hours depending on size and services. We'll give you a time estimate with your quote.

Do you clean on weekends and holidays?

Do you offer regular / recurring cleaning?

Yes, we offer scheduled cleaning for homes and commercial spaces — weekly, bi-weekly or monthly, at your convenience.

What if I'm not satisfied with the cleaning?

Tell us right away and we'll come back and re-clean the area at no extra charge — your satisfaction is our priority.
