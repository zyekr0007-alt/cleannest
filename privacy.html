Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

CleanNest (“we”, “us”) operates cleannest.in and provides professional cleaning services in Jalandhar, Punjab, India. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, phone number, email address, service details, property size and address, and any other details you share when you contact us by phone, WhatsApp, email, the website booking form, or Instagram.
  • Technical information: this website is a static site hosted on a third-party platform, which may log standard, non-personal data such as IP address and browser type for security and performance purposes. We do not run our own analytics or tracking cookies on this site.

How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries, prepare quotes and confirm bookings.
  • To deliver cleaning services you have requested.
  • To send service-related messages (confirmation, follow-ups, rebooking reminders) via WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • To improve our services and customer care.

WhatsApp and the booking form

When you use our booking form, your details open a pre-filled WhatsApp message on your own device and are sent to us by you through WhatsApp. The form itself does not store or transmit your data to any server we control. WhatsApp messages are subject to WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy policy.

Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We only share details with third parties where necessary to deliver a service you requested (for example, payment or communication platforms), or where required by law.

Data retention & security

We keep enquiry and booking records only as long as needed for business purposes (quotes, bookings, warranties and follow-ups). We take reasonable steps to protect your information and limit access to staff who need it to serve you.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, request correction or deletion, or ask us to stop contacting you. Message us on WhatsApp at +91 76100 00654 or email cleannestclub@gmail.com.

Contact
